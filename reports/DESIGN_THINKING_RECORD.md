# Design-thinking record

Discover, define, and ideate outputs are recorded in `DISCOVERY_REVIEW.md`,
`RESEARCH_DEFINITION.md`, and `EXPERIMENT_DESIGN_DECISION.md`. Prototype and
test use one deterministic implementation and frozen outputs. Refine is
limited to interpretation: no post-outcome tuning, feature search, or test-set
redesign is permitted in this run.

The chosen tests deliberately include an observational-equivalence pair in the
model organisms. A method that assigns a distinct hidden producer to those
otherwise indistinguishable artifacts is therefore demonstrably overclaiming.
